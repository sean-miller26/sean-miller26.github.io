Contributor Dashboard

Volunteers were passionate, but they didn’t always know how their contributions added up. Stats were scattered across the platform, inconsistent, and hard to trust. Our goal? Give volunteers a single, clear view of their impact, and make it motivating.

The result is a redesigned stats page that turns passive volunteering into an engaging experience. Volunteers can see their progress, understand their contributions, and even celebrate milestones. Educators and teams can track group impact effortlessly, while the platform benefits from less support work and more autonomous users.

8 months

Team

Platform

Web & Mobile

Industry

My Role

I led the design from start to finish: conducting UX research, sketching wireframes, and coordinating handoff between developers, marketing, and branding. I also oversaw the launch and collected user feedback to ensure the design truly met both technical and experience goals.

Problems

Inconsistent stats

Users weren’t sure they could trust the numbers.

Low motivation

Without clear progress indicators, casual users had little incentive to keep contributing.

Limited self-serve options

Educators and corporate partners had to rely on our team for access to stats.

Goals and Principles

Our focus was on building tools that motivate users through clarity, ownership, and autonomy, while helping the team reduce repetitive support work. The guiding principles:

Modular

Flexible components that could serve multiple use cases.

Streamlined

Clean, easy-to-digest visuals.

Trusted

Accurate, transparent stats users could rely on.

Autonomous

Users could access and share their own data without asking for help.

Research

We talked to educators, super-users, and brand-new volunteers to understand what motivated them and where they got stuck. I also analyzed other platforms’ stats systems and reviewed internal React components to find patterns that minimize cognitive load while maximizing clarity.

Key insights:

  • Volunteers wanted autonomy: the ability to generate certificates and access stats independently.
  • Social proof inspired participation: group and classroom stats encouraged friendly competition and collaboration.

Testing

Before this project, testing was informal and reactive. I introduced a structured beta testing process, making it easier to identify pain points, verify data accuracy, and confirm that certificate generation worked seamlessly.

Launch and Impact

When we rolled out the stats page, certificate generator, and groups dashboard, blog posts alone received over 12,000 views. Volunteers started bookmarking their dashboards, an unexpected sign of high perceived value.

Other results:

  • Support tickets around volunteer certification dropped significantly.
  • Group creation skyrocketed as volunteers, teachers, and organizations used stats to track shared impact.
  • Group classifications quickly reached millions, showing that clear, shared stats keep people engaged longer.

Outcome

In the end, the new stats system did more than visualize data, it reshaped how volunteers saw themselves within the larger mission. By giving volunteers a clear view of their progress and impact, we turned numbers into something personal and meaningful. Volunteers began returning more often, not because they had to, but because they wanted to.

For our team, the redesign eased the amount of support tickets and proved that when users are trusted with their own data, they thrive. Classrooms and organizations began using the tools to celebrate shared milestones, creating real motivation and recognition.

Our finished product is a system that built trust, strengthened community, and made volunteering feel visible and rewarding. It showed that clarity and ownership aren't just design goals, they’re what turn participation into purpose.

Lessons Learned

As a team, we learned the value of cross-functional alignment early and often—especially when a project touches multiple aspects of the platform, from content to infrastructure. By working closely with developers, marketing, and internal researchers, we ensured that design decisions were technically feasible, on-brand, and rooted in real user needs. We also realized how critical it is to balance storytelling with clarity; while we wanted to inspire users, we learned through feedback and testing that users first need to understand what Zooniverse is before they can feel motivated to participate.

This project helped us rethink how we communicate value across the platform and reinforced the importance of designing for both new and returning audiences.
