About

Sole UX Designer for Zooniverse, the world’s largest citizen science platform with nearly 3 million users. I design scalable systems for complex, mission-driven products. I built the Zooniverse design system from the ground up and design data heavy dashboards that make scientific collaboration easier to see and understand.

My background spans digital product design, physical fabrication, and public engagement, giving me an unusually hands-on perspective on how people interact in both digital and physical spaces. I bring the same design thinking to interactive museum exhibits at Adler Planetarium as I do to complex web interfaces.

When I’m not designing, I’m probably on a baseball diamond, digging through record crates or making music with friends.
